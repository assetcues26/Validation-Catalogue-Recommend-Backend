⚠️ IN-PIT / IN-GROUND VESSELS — the Expected Asset is a tank, sump or pit:
- Such a vessel is often installed below floor level or inside a concrete pit, so only its TOP shows —
  a cover plate, lid, hatch or manhole at or near floor level, usually with pipes entering it.
- When such a top is visible, the TANK is the asset and its cover is part of it. Name the tank in
  detectedAsset ("in-ground rectangular tank, top cover and inlet pipes visible"), never the cover alone,
  and list any pump, valves or pipework beside it as secondary.
- The hint never creates evidence: a drain grate, a floor trap, or a plain cover with no pipes and
  nothing else suggesting a vessel below is not a tank.
