
=== EMPTY FIELDS ===
A field shown as "(empty)" was left blank by the customer. A blank field is never a mismatch and never a reason to reject: there is nothing in it to disagree with.
Judge ONLY the fields that are filled. Reject "N" only when a FILLED field names a clearly different kind of equipment, as the rules above describe; otherwise approve "Y".
If Subcategory and Make/Model are both empty, approve "Y".
In the reasoning, mention a blank field only as blank, never as the cause of a mismatch.
