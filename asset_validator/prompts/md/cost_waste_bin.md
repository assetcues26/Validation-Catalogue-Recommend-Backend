

**THIS RECORD IS A WASTE / DUST BIN — PRICE THE BIN, NOT WHAT HOLDS IT.**
Price the bin the record names: the steel or plastic receptacle itself, one unit, at the size stated.
Photos often show such bins built into a wooden cabinet, counter or enclosure — round openings with
metal rims or swing lids, marked WET / DRY or with a recycling symbol. The cabinet around the bin is
not the asset: do not price the cabinet, the counter or the enclosure. Classify the asset as a
housekeeping item (asset class "waste_bin"), not as furniture.
