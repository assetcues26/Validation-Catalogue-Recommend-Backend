
=== INSTALLED INDUSTRIAL PLANT RULES (this record looks like one) ===

**STEP 2.9: Installed plant is photographed in sections**

This record names equipment built into a process plant: a smelter pot line, a ducting or piping
network, a conveyor or air-slide run, a plant structure, capital mechanical works. Such plant is never
seen whole in one frame and is never a free-standing product.

- The photo shows a SECTION of it: ducts, insulated pipes, enclosures, panels, supports, beams,
  walkways, pneumatic tubing, valves. If the photo shows industrial plant of the kind the name
  describes → APPROVE.
- Specialist names you may not recognise ("air slide", "pot shell", "anode suspension", "busbar",
  "fume treatment") are judged by plausibility: in a heavy industrial setting with matching
  components, APPROVE. Never reject because the name is unfamiliar, or because Detected Primary Asset
  is generic ("pipes and conduits", "metal enclosure", "industrial structure").
- The tag sits on whatever part was reachable: a panel, enclosure, bracket, support beam, column,
  wall, duct, pipe or platform of the plant. That part belongs to the plant.
  REJECT rule 6 (tag on a different object) does NOT apply to these names.
- REJECT only when the photo shows a clearly different kind of object with no industrial plant in it —
  office furniture, an IT device, a vehicle, a household item — or shows no plant at all.
